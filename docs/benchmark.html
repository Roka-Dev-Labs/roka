Benchmark

10 Nov 2026 rerun of shipped pruneText (prune_file(path, budget=4000), no query) on 15 public LogHub 2k files. 30,003 lines / 4,178,984 characters. Zookeeper excluded.

MetricResult
Mean lines removed98.3%
Character compression70× (4.18M → 59.7K)
Files with a crash-regex line that still had one11 / 11

What this measures

Retention under budget — line removal, and whether a line matching ERROR|FATAL|CRITICAL|Exception|Traceback|panic survived a 4,000-character pack. It does not prove the tool finds the right evidence for an arbitrary question, or that agents debug better with the slice. This run passed no query.

Full methodology, Thunderbird before/after, and explicit non-claims: research.html. Per-dataset table: testing.html.
